Questions

FAQ

Do I need to provide anything?

No. We bring everything — chair, tools, vacuum, and extension cord. All we need is a designated space to set up.

How many people can you cut in a day?

Typically 8–10 people for a full office day, depending on the schedule. Exact capacity is confirmed at booking.

How does the office booking link work?

After confirming your office day, we send you a Calendly link. You forward it to your team — they pick their own time slots from what's available. No back-and-forth required.

Do you serve areas outside Metro Atlanta?

Metro Atlanta is our primary service area. We'll travel further for weddings, events, or larger bookings — reach out to discuss.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation details are included in your booking confirmation. We ask for reasonable notice so we can offer the slot to someone else.
